      📖 Game Guide

      🏗️ Building Tools

      ResidentialHouses citizens. Needs road + power. Generates waste.
      CommercialShops that boost Wellbeing and income.
      FarmingOrganic goods. Great for Sustainability and Health.
      Textile FactoryClothing from raw fabric. Pollutes slightly.
      Tech FactoryElectronics. High income, some pollution.
      Steel FactoryHeavy industry. Big money, heavy pollution.
      AutomotiveVehicles. High income. Needs steel + electronics.
      Recycling CenterWaste to recycled materials. Massive Sustainability!

      ⚡ Energy & Infrastructure

      Solar PanelClean energy. Boosts Sustainability. No pollution.
      Wind TurbineBest clean energy. Highest Sustainability boost.
      Hydro PlantPowerful renewable. Good Wellbeing + Sustainability.
      Waste-to-EnergyBurns waste for power. Slightly hurts Health.
      RoadConnects buildings. Required for buildings to function!

      🏛️ Public Services

      ParkSmall Wellbeing and Health boost. Cheap.
      SchoolMajor Education boost. Better economy.
      HospitalBig Health boost. Essential for citizens.
      Awareness CenterEducation + Sustainability. Circular economy.
      MRFSorts waste for recycling. Big Sustainability.
      Water TreatmentCleans wastewater. Boosts Sustainability.

      📊 Score System

      WellbeingCitizen happiness. Commercial zones, parks, employment.
      EducationKnowledge level. Schools and awareness centers.
      HealthPublic health. Hospitals help, factories hurt.
      SustainabilityHow green. Recycling, renewables boost. Factories decrease.
      Management ScoreAverage of all four. Key metric for winning!

      🔧 Toolbar Buttons

      SelectClick buildings to view info and upgrade.
      BulldozeDemolish buildings. No refunds!
      PausePause simulation. Plan without pressure.
      ObjectivesToggle scenario objectives & tips panel.
      ResourcesView raw materials, products, waste and trade.

      💡 Key Tips

      • Buildings need road access AND energy to develop.
      • Factories produce goods but also pollution and waste.
      • Use Recycling Centers to turn waste into recycled materials.
      • Balance economy (factories) with environment (renewables, recycling).
      • The Management Score is the average of all four scores.
      • Click a building to see details and upgrade it for better output.
